what is ruby diamond ring ?

A ruby diamond ring combines the beauty of a ruby, known for its vibrant red color symbolizing passion and love, with a diamond, representing eternity and brilliance. Rubies are celebrated for their durability and deep red hue, often associated with strength and protection, while diamonds are renowned for their unmatched hardness and classic elegance.
